Consulting delivery model

A controlled assessment lifecycle—from rules of engagement to verified closure.

I treat an assessment as a managed security workstream, not a scanner export: scope control, attack-surface prioritization, evidence integrity, severity governance, stakeholder communication, remediation coordination, and explicit retest criteria.

Review sanitized engagement outcomes →
  1. 01

    Control scope and rules of engagement

    Define assets, identities, test windows, constraints, evidence handling, escalation paths, and success criteria before testing begins.

  2. 02

    Model the attack surface

    Map trust boundaries, roles, objects, data flows, state transitions, and high-value workflows to create focused threat hypotheses.

  3. 03

    Validate and chain exploitation

    Use manual testing, source-assisted reasoning, negative controls, and safe PoCs to prove exploitability and realistic attack paths.

  4. 04

    Govern findings and risk decisions

    Deduplicate evidence, calibrate severity, explain business impact, identify root cause, and communicate clearly with technical and non-technical stakeholders.

  5. 05

    Drive remediation and retest

    Translate findings into corrective controls, validate the fix, test for bypasses and regressions, and close the issue against defined evidence.
