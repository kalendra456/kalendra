How I work

Evidence before severity. Impact before noise.

A professional security assessment is more than a list of scanner findings. I focus on reproducibility, exploitability, realistic attack paths, and remediation that engineering teams can act on.

See this approach in practice →
  1. 01

    Map the surface

    Understand assets, identities, trust boundaries, data flows, and high-value workflows.

  2. 02

    Build abuse cases

    Test how controls behave across roles, objects, state transitions, and chained actions.

  3. 03

    Validate real impact

    Reproduce safely, remove false positives, and demonstrate the practical consequence.

  4. 04

    Make remediation usable

    Deliver concise evidence, risk context, root cause, and developer-ready corrective guidance.
